Editorial Policy

Bezeru is committed to independent and transparent watch journalism.

Independence

All editorial content is created without influence from brands, manufacturers, or retailers unless explicitly stated. Brand relationships do not determine coverage.

Product Access

If watches are loaned for review or events are attended at the invitation of brands, this does not guarantee coverage nor influence editorial opinion.

Accuracy

We strive to ensure technical and historical accuracy. When errors are identified, corrections will be made transparently.

No Pay-to-Play

Bezeru does not accept payment in exchange for editorial coverage.

Clear Separation of Editorial and Commercial Content

If sponsored or paid content is ever published, it will be clearly labeled as such.
