ABOUT BEZERU

A watch notebook built on clarity.

BEZERU focuses on what changed, what didn’t, and what matters on the wrist. We map modern releases to their vintage references, highlight Gulf and Middle East editions, and keep the critique grounded in design over hype.

Our point of view

Watches carry history, culture, and engineering in a space smaller than your palm. BEZERU is where contemporary drops meet their vintage roots, where Arabic dials and regional makers are treated with the seriousness they deserve, and where the focus stays on what you actually wear.
