About BEZERU

BEZERU is a modern + vintage watch publication focused on what actually changes on-wrist: proportion, ergonomics, design decisions, and movement choices. We connect new releases to the vintage references that shaped them—without hype.

Our niche

We explain watches through design. If it wears better, reads cleaner, or is engineered more honestly, we’ll tell you. If it’s marketing noise, we’ll tell you that too.
