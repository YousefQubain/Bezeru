About the founder

Hi, I’m Yousef — and BEZERU is my watch notebook.

I started BEZERU because I’ve always loved watches for what they really are: design in motion. A dial can carry history, culture, and engineering in a space smaller than your palm — and the best pieces reward you the more you look.

I also wanted a place that treats Gulf and Middle Eastern watch culture seriously. From Arabic dials and regional commissions to collectors shaping modern taste, the influence is real — and often under-covered. BEZERU connects modern releases to their vintage roots, highlights underdogs, and keeps the focus on what matters on the wrist.

Editorial focus

Gulf editions, Arabic dials, design critique, and smart buying advice—clean and practical.

Why BEZERU

Because the region’s taste and history deserve real coverage—not a footnote.
