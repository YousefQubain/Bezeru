ABOUT

Hi, I’m Yousef — and BEZERU is my watch notebook.

I started BEZERU because I love watches for what they really are: design in motion. A dial can carry culture, engineering, and history in a space smaller than your palm — and the best pieces reward you the more you look.

BEZERU is also a place to treat Middle East influence seriously — from Arabic dials and regional commissions to the collectors and tastes shaping modern releases. Alongside underdogs and vintage references, I’ll cover the releases that matter and the stories that are usually under-covered.
