About BEZERU

BEZERU is a digital watch publication connecting modern releases with vintage context—focused on design, features, and on-wrist reality.

What we publish

  • Feature Breakdown: What changed and why it matters.
  • Release Radar: The best drops you might have missed.
  • Vintage Spotlight: Classics that still teach modern watchmaking a lesson.
  • Design Critique: Proportions, typography, finishing, ergonomics.

Schedule

Twice a week: Tuesday + Friday.
