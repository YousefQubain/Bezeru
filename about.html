About
ABOUT

Hi, I’m Yousef — and BEZERU is my watch notebook.

I started BEZERU because I’ve always loved watches for what they really are: design in motion. A dial can carry history, culture, and engineering in a space smaller than your palm — and the best pieces reward you the more you look.

BEZERU is about watches in general: underdogs, new competition, and vintage context — with real attention to Gulf and Middle East influence. From Arabic dials and regional commissions to collectors shaping modern taste, the influence is real — and often under-covered. My goal is simple: connect releases to roots, talk honestly about design decisions, and keep the focus on what matters on the wrist.
