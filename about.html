Hi, I’m Yousef — and BEZERU is my watch notebook.

I started BEZERU because I’ve always loved watches for what they really are: design in motion. A dial can carry history, culture, and engineering in a space smaller than your palm — and the best pieces reward you the more you look.

I also wanted a place that treats Gulf and Middle Eastern watch culture seriously. From Arabic dials and regional commissions to the collectors shaping modern taste, the influence is real — and often under-covered. BEZERU connects modern releases to their vintage roots and keeps the focus on what matters on the wrist.
