About BEZERU

BEZERU started as a personal project driven by curiosity — not hype. I wanted a place to talk about watches the way collectors actually experience them: through design, fit, culture, and long-term value, not just specs or brand prestige.

I’m Yousef, and my interest sits between modern releases, vintage context, and the often-overlooked relationship between watchmaking and the Middle East — from Arabic dials to regional commissions and cultural preferences that quietly shape global design. BEZERU exists to explore those connections, highlight underdogs, and explain why certain watches matter beyond marketing.
