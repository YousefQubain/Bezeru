ABOUT

Hi, I’m Yousef — and BEZERU is my watch notebook.

I started BEZERU to talk about watches the way collectors actually experience them: on the wrist, in motion, and in context — design, history, engineering, and culture without hype.

What BEZERU covers

BEZERU is built around four ideas: (1) modern releases make more sense when you trace their vintage roots, (2) underdog brands deserve serious attention when the design and execution are right, (3) buying guides should be practical — proportion, value, and wearability over buzz, and (4) the Gulf / Middle East has shaped watch taste and production for decades (Arabic dials, commissions, gold culture, gifting traditions) — and that influence is often under-covered.

This project is also personal: I’m drawn to watches as “design in motion” — tiny objects that carry real engineering and real culture. If you’re into the details that matter on the wrist, you’ll feel at home here.
