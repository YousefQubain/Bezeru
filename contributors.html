Contributor Guidelines

Bezeru welcomes contributors who share an interest in watch design, engineering, history, and culture.

What We Publish

We are interested in:

  • Brand histories and design analysis
  • Independent and emerging watchmakers
  • Technical explanations written clearly
  • Industry trends and cultural commentary

Style & Tone

Writing should be clear, analytical, and respectful. Promotional language should be avoided.

Original Work

All submissions must be original and not published elsewhere.

Rights

By submitting content, contributors grant Bezeru the right to publish and edit submissions for clarity and length.

How to Pitch

Please send:

  • A short bio
  • Article idea or outline
  • Writing samples if available
