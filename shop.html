Before you inquire.

Is BEZERU a marketplace?

No. BEZERU is an editorial introduction channel, not a payment platform or escrow service.

What should buyers include?

Reference, budget, location, condition preference, timing, and whether box or papers matter.

What should sellers include?

Full photos, reference, condition notes, service history, ownership details, price target, and location.
