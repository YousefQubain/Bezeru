What we accept

Modern, vintage, and independent watches with clear ownership history, complete photos, and fair market pricing. We celebrate honest descriptions and well-cared-for pieces.

How the BEZERU shop works

Step 1

Submit your watch

Share the reference, condition notes, service history, and a full photo set. We will help you price the piece based on recent market data and comparable listings.

Step 2

Editorial review

We verify details, tighten copy, and highlight what makes the watch special. Every listing is presented with the same care as our editorial features.

Step 3

Connect with buyers

Interested collectors can reach out through BEZERU for secure introductions. You stay in control of final terms while we provide support through the process.

List a watch

Start your listing

Email shop@bezeru.com with the model reference, asking price, your location, and 8-12 high resolution photos (dial, case, clasp, movement, and accessories). We will respond within two business days with next steps.

By submitting, you confirm the watch is owned by you and that all information provided is accurate.
