WATCH BRANDS

Brands

A clear editorial map of the brands shaping modern collecting, from foundational houses to value leaders.

Rolex

Rolex

Tool-first icons turned global benchmarks. Focus on proportion, dial era, and condition quality over hype cycles.

Omega

Omega

Space history, diver credibility, and strong movement development. Speedmaster and Seamaster remain core references.

Patek

Patek Philippe

High-end complications with restraint and continuity. A benchmark for long-horizon collecting and legacy value.

A.P.

Audemars Piguet

Royal Oak architecture that reshaped sporty luxury. Case finishing and bracelet execution are central to the appeal.

Cartier

Cartier

Shape-led watchmaking with historic clarity. Tank and Santos remain best-in-class for elegant everyday wear.

Seiko

Seiko

From reliable entry points to Grand Seiko finishing, Seiko anchors value and depth in one ecosystem.

Tudor

Tudor

Robust, vintage-informed sports watches with modern sizing and strong day-to-day practicality.
